Method & data quality

40 records parsed. Required fields, dates and numeric ranges checked. 0 source warning(s); 4 records meet review rules. All records are included, including zero-unit records and any duplicates.

  • Gross sales = units × unit price. Discount value = gross sales × discount rate. Net sales = gross sales − discount value.
  • Decimal arithmetic retains full precision until display, rounded half up to pennies. Displayed line amounts can differ by pennies from totals calculated before rounding. No tax, shipping, cost or refund treatment is inferred.
  • Weighted discount = total discount value ÷ total gross sales. Sales share = group net sales ÷ overall net sales. Week-on-week = (current − previous) ÷ previous, only for adjacent recorded weeks. Zero denominators appear as n/a.
  • Review rules: zero units, discounts ≥20%, or positive quantities outside 0.5–2× the product median, provided the product has at least five positive records. Thresholds are transparent heuristics, not statistical tests or company policy.
  • Abbreviated first names sharing an initial and surname are flagged as possible aliases; no names are merged. Exact duplicates are retained and flagged because no transaction ID establishes whether they are errors.
  • Each CSV row is a sales record, not a verified order or customer. No conversion rate, order value, profit, target attainment or causal effect is inferred.
